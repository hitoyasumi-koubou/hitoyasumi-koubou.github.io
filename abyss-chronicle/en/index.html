PROLOGUE

How it begins

Ten years ago, the old guildmaster set out for the Hollow Deep and never came back.

All that remained was the Dawnlight, a guild with a faded sign, and one mysterious book: the Chronicle.

You were once an apprentice chronicler. Now you take up the sign as the new Master.

Send your adventurers out, and when enough time has passed, they come home with treasure and stories.

HOW TO PLAY

How to play

  1. 1

    Send them out

    Pick a destination on the world map, form a party, and send it off.

  2. 2

    Wait

    Time keeps flowing while the app is closed, and the adventure moves on.

  3. 3

    Read and grow

    Use the treasure they bring back to strengthen your adventurers and facilities, and read their journals and the story.

FEATURES

Features

01

Adventures go on while you’re away

Time keeps flowing while the app is closed, so expeditions, crafting, training, and research keep moving. Open the game a few times a day in your spare moments, collect the results, and give your next orders.

Auto-repeat rests once 4 hours have passed since you last opened the game (gems or the Monthly Pass can extend this), so check in now and then.

02

Adventurers and their Bonds

Reckless, Cautious, Joker, Cynic: each personality changes how an adventurer talks and how their journal entries read.

The more they adventure together, the deeper their Bonds grow, and certain pairs unlock Bond Episodes of their own.

03

Nina at the front desk

In the Hall, Nina, the apprentice receptionist, shares cozy chats with your adventurers.

Talk with her and keep sending out expeditions to build your Friendship with Nina, and watch her grow into a full-fledged receptionist.

04

A main story across the continent

A story told through conversations with character portraits. Follow the trail of the lost guildmaster and uncover the mystery of the Hollow Deep.

05

The Hundred-Lamp Tower (100 floors)

An old tower where you send in a squad one floor at a time and relight each floor’s lost lamp. Higher floors are tougher, and each floor has an element and conditions that reward the right personalities, Bonds, and classes.

Drive back the Warden every 10 floors to earn a legendary weapon found only in the tower and read one more record left behind in it.

06

A world map and a different giant boss each week

Pick destinations on the world map and send out your parties. Each week, a different giant World Boss appears: a long battle where you send parties again and again over the whole week.

The longer a sortie lasts, the more damage it deals, but the risk of injury rises too.

  • Six elements

    Fire, Water, Wind, Earth, Light, and Dark. Match class and weapon elements to each tower floor and to the World Boss’s weak points.

  • Daily goals and the Growth Pass

    Daily and Weekly Missions, achievements, and titles. The Growth Pass runs in 4-week seasons: the more you play, the higher your tier.

  • Google Play Games

    Sign-in is optional. Leaderboards for the Hundred-Lamp Tower and the World Boss, achievements, and cloud save.

  • Grow your guild at your own pace

    Craft and enhance gear, expand facilities, grow each adventurer’s skill tree, and change their classes. Little by little, build the guild that’s yours.

ADVENTURERS

The adventurers

Some join you through the story, and you meet others mainly through Special Invitations. Each personality changes how an adventurer talks and how their journal entries read.

  • NinaReceptionist

    The apprentice receptionist at the Dawnlight. She sends the adventurers off and waits for them to come home.

    “Broom, check. Rag, check. Front-desk smile... check!”

  • TotoHunter

    A forest-raised archer brought up by his woodcutter grandfather, with a sharp eye for anything edible.

    “Master, what’s on the menu today?”

  • MiraPriest

    From the same village as Toto. Her apothecary mother taught her herbs and simple healing prayers.

    “Toto, you got hurt again!”

  • RineMage

    The only daughter of a mage who entered the Hollow Deep ten years ago. She is looking for her father’s name.

    “My father’s name should be in that book.”

  • ElwenHunter

    Guardian of the Amberwood Maze, an elf who spent a hundred-odd years patrolling the forest.

    “The forest is crying. That’s reason enough.”

  • BaldurKnight

    The guild’s former Lieutenant, who built the Dawnlight into a great guild alongside the old guildmaster.

    “...So the lamp never went out.”

  • OswaldPriest

    A priest of the Church of the Holy Bell who learned a secret hidden in the cathedral archives.

    “Heaven is silent. So I do the talking.”

  • SigurdSwordsman

    A young swordsman from the slums of Verna who made his name with his blade alone.

    “The scales I believed in were never meant to weigh like this.”
